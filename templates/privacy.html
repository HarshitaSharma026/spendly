Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when creating an account, including your name and email address. We also store the expense records you create — such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — to provide the core functionality of Spendly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly. This includes displaying your expense history, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third-party services for marketing or analytics purposes. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) to operate the app; these providers are contractually obligated to keep your data confidential.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly and transparently.
